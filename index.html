2How is it graded?

How the grade is rounded at the end. "0.1" turns 6.36 into 6.4; "0.5" turns 6.36 into 6.5; "1" gives whole numbers. Halves are rounded up (6.25 → 6.3).

Most UvA exams are passed with a 5.5; some hard courses use 5.0. Some exams instead say "you need at least x points". "Exact grade" means a 5.45 is a fail, even if it would be rounded to 5.5.
